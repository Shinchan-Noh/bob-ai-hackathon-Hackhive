
# P1: Clinical Trial Risk Monitor & Protocol Deviation Detector

## Problem Statement

A major clinical trial has 5,000+ patient visits across 200+ sites. Protocol deviations—missed visits, wrong dosing, banned medications—go undetected until the FDA audit.

A single rejected submission delays drug approval by 6–12 months and costs $50–100M. Risk managers need real-time visibility into which sites are highest risk before problems escalate.

## Our Challenge

Build an IBM Bob solution that:

1. Compares patient records against the protocol specification.
2. Classifies each deviation by severity (ICH E6 GCP: major, minor, administrative).
3. Scores site-level risk using leading indicators.
4. Generates CAPA-ready reports with recommended mitigations.
